 # genaijourney

## Overview
A long-term transition from traditional frontend-focused development toward intelligent systems, Generative AI, agentic workflows, and human-centered AI products.

The journey is heavily project-oriented instead of theory-first.

The goal is not:
- collecting certificates
- blindly consuming tutorials
- memorizing concepts without application

The goal is:
- building real systems
- understanding AI through creation
- developing intuition through experimentation
- becoming capable of building end-to-end AI products

---

## Current Direction

The learning direction intentionally prioritizes:
- practical implementation
- shipping projects
- product thinking
- system design
- experimentation
- intuition building

The approach is:
> build first, deepen later.

---

## Why The Shift Happened

Originally the focus was heavily frontend-oriented:
- interfaces
- motion systems
- creative frontend engineering
- interaction design

Over time, interest shifted toward:
- intelligent systems
- recommendation engines
- emotional AI
- prediction systems
- agentic workflows
- memory-aware systems

The transition happened because:
> static interfaces started feeling incomplete without intelligence behind them.
