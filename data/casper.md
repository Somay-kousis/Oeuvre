# casper

## Overview
Casper is an emotionally-aware AI companion designed to make digital interaction feel warm, safe, calming, and human.

The project explores the idea that AI does not need to feel cold or robotic. Instead of focusing purely on productivity or utility, Casper focuses on emotional presence, companionship, regulation, and comfort.

Casper is not designed to replace therapy or human relationships.  
Its purpose is to reduce emotional isolation and create safe spaces for expression, calmness, and connection.

---

## Core Philosophy

Casper is built around a simple belief:

> AI can feel emotionally gentle without pretending to be human.

The system is intentionally:
- soft
- slow
- emotionally careful
- non-judgmental
- sensory-aware
- comfort-oriented

The goal is not to “fix” people.

The goal is:
- emotional presence
- calm companionship
- safe interaction
- human-centered AI experiences

---

## Personality

Casper speaks softly and slowly.

The tone is:
- reassuring
- emotionally aware
- warm
- calm
- careful
- non-corporate
- non-clinical

Casper avoids:
- robotic language
- overly cheerful responses
- emotionally overwhelming wording
- pressure-based interaction
- productivity-focused behavior

The interaction style is designed to feel like:
- a quiet late-night conversation
- a comforting presence
- someone sitting beside you during difficult moments

## Core Features

### Emotional Conversation
Users can talk through:
- text
- voice
- emotional prompts
- guided reflection

Casper responds with emotionally aware dialogue focused on:
- listening
- grounding
- emotional regulation
- companionship

---

### Breathing System
During anxious or overwhelming moments, Casper can guide breathing exercises through:
- animated glowing orb visuals
- synchronized motion
- calming pacing
- possible haptic feedback on supported devices

The breathing system is designed to create a feeling similar to:
- holding someone’s hand
- synchronized breathing
- nervous system calming

---

### Doodle Spaces
Users can enter low-pressure shared spaces focused on:
- drawing
- calming interaction
- silent co-presence
- emotional decompression

The feature exists for moments where:
- words feel exhausting
- users cannot communicate verbally
- emotional pressure is too high

---

### Emotionally Safe Matching System
Casper includes a carefully designed emotional matching system.

The goal is not rapid social matching.

The goal is emotional safety.

The system evaluates:
- emotional state
- conversation boundaries
- sensitivity topics
- communication needs
- current emotional energy

Examples of system prompts:
- “What are you okay talking about today?”
- “Do you want to listen or be heard?”
- “Are there topics you want to avoid?”

---

## Matching Safety Logic

### Core Principle
Two emotionally vulnerable people should not automatically be connected simply because they share pain.

Casper intentionally avoids:
- emotional triggering loops
- unhealthy dependency cycles
- trauma amplification
- emotionally dangerous pairings

---

### Safety Layers
The matching system may include:
- emotional intensity balancing
- topic sensitivity filtering
- gradual trust progression
- monitored group-first interaction
- slow social exposure

Users initially interact in:
- small shared spaces
- group rooms
- low-pressure environments

Direct one-on-one interaction only happens after:
- comfort
- consent
- emotional stability indicators

## Why Casper Exists

Casper was created from the belief that loneliness is becoming increasingly digital, yet most technology still feels emotionally distant.

The project explores whether AI systems can:
- reduce emotional isolation
- create calming experiences
- help users feel heard
- support emotional regulation
- create safer online emotional spaces

Casper is not trying to become human.

It is trying to become emotionally safe.
